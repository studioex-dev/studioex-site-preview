Look at me

To look, regardless of personality, colour, social class or gender, to show that, despite differences, everyone deserves to be seen.

Olha Pra Mim, Portuguese for ‘look at me’, is a project by the photographer Thi Santos. It began in 2014, when Thi noticed how alike family and friends were in the way they reacted to those three words. The idea is to help people connect with their own humanity through looks, without any kind of judgement.

The artist

Thi Santos

A photographer from Pernambuco, based in São Paulo, with a sensitive, refined eye for light and touches of colour. Published in Elle, L’Officiel Italia, Vogue and Claudia.

The Olha Pra Mim series, and the book of portraits that came from it in 2021, has portrayed names from across Brazilian culture, among them Caetano Veloso, Elza Soares, Gilberto Gil, Preta Gil, Ivete Sangalo, Criolo, Anitta and Jô Soares.
