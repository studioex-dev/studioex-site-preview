The idea

Eight runners. One marathon. 80 kg, shared until everyone carries 10 kg.

A performative artwork and series of short films by Blueprint, produced by Studio EX, to bring awareness, action and funding to the mental health crisis.

The run starts with 80 kg. At 10, 16 and 24 km the load is halved and shared, until all eight runners carry 10 kg each.
