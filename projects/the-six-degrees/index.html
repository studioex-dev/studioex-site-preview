The idea

The world is smaller than we think, and this artwork proves it.

The Six Degrees is a real-time, interactive artwork that maps how every person is connected by six steps or fewer. It grows by invitation, London first, then becomes a show you walk through.

How it works

  1. 1

    Join by invitation

    Your link adds you to the map, next to the person who sent it.

  2. 2

    Watch the map grow

    Every invitation shows how few steps stand between any two people.

  3. 3

    Step inside

    A touring show, projection-mapped, with rooms that know you’re there.
