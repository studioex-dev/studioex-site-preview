Our story

Where it comes from

Small World is a participation platform: one person joins, invites the next, and everyone can see what grew.

Anyone you can name is aboutsix steps away.

In 1967, Stanley Milgram asked people to pass a letter towards a stranger, only through someone they knew. The chains that arrived took about six steps. He called it the small-world problem, and we named the platform after it.

Small World makes that chain visible. Every person who joins is a leaf, every invitation a branch, and every world a living picture of who it reached and what it made possible.

What we stand for

Four values

  • Visible

    You can see what you started. Every branch is drawn.

  • Proven

    We count what is checked. Pledges are shown, never counted.

  • Private

    We show how many, never who. A place with fewer than 50 people shows as fewer than 50.

  • Together

    Nobody acts alone. Every world is grown by many hands.
