The name

A parlour is the room a house keeps for its guests, and it is named after what happens inside it: from the French parler, to speak.

For 700 years it has been the room set apart: the monastery room where the silence lifted, the Victorian best room with the piano, the West Indian front room with the radiogram.

Parlour puts you beside each other.
