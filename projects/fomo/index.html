The open call art series

Touring events · London · since 2025

How it works

FOMO is Studio EX’s open call art series: an open call, a themed group show and a party, run as numbered editions. Applying is free; artists who are chosen pay a fee set by the size of their work and the venue.

  1. [ 01 ]

    The open call

    Free to apply, around a theme. If you’re chosen, the fee depends on the size of your work and the venue.

  2. [ 02 ]

    The group show

    The chosen work, hung together in one show.

  3. [ 03 ]

    The party

    Opening night. A night you don’t want to miss.
