Mutually Reassured Destruction

Two letters apart. In the old idea, nobody acts because everyone is armed. In ours, nobody acts because everyone is reassured — by green reports, kind words and pretty pictures.

Every piece holds both

The story

Nine scenes, one camera move each.

The piece opens calm, then looks closer, scene by scene, from one living ground to a forest that everyone grows. Each scene has its own move, used once.

Stills from the prototype · sample data

  1. S00 · Calm

    Enjoy nature

    A calm, blurred living ground under one glass sheet, and two numbers: the people in the forest, and the forest lost since you arrived.

  2. S01 · Split

    Look closer

    The same moment, split in two: the calm on one side, felled stumps up close on the other.

  3. S02 · Unroll

    The record, in rings

    Every year since 1850 as a growth ring, coloured by its temperature. The rings unroll into warming stripes.

  4. S03 · Pull back

    The cut

    A ring is cut. The stump joins a field of stumps, and from orbit the cleared forest shows its shape.

  5. S04 · Drop

    Where it falls

    Each year’s tropical forest loss since 2002 drops onto the map as a square, at true size.

  6. S05 · Draw

    The promises

    Three promises are planted on the record of fossil CO₂ emissions. The line keeps rising after each one.

  7. S06 · Branch

    The forecast

    The temperature record becomes a trunk that branches into three possible futures to 2100.

  8. S07 · Fall

    The turn

    A seed falls from the 1.5 °C branch and sprouts. Nobody acts alone.

  9. S08 · Rise

    The forest

    One mark, then its branch, its tree, a grove and the whole forest. Looking down a trunk, the forest keeps its own rings: one for every 1,000 people.

How it works

An artwork that grows with you.

Every person who takes part adds to it, so the piece grows as more people join. It runs on Small World, our participation platform.

References

Where the look comes from.

MRD’s brand guidelines set the look with one Instagram story and a handful of references. The living and wounded worlds are shown here in documentary photos we can publish, each credited and its place named. The design references are other designers’ work, shown as influences, not as the campaign’s images.

The core reference

One Instagram story sets the whole look. MRD takes four things from it.

  1. 01A blurred, living ground: nature out of focus, calm first.
  2. 02A frosted glass sheet: everything sits on glass, never straight on the photo.
  3. 03Type turned on its side: the big word runs vertical, labels run up the edge.
  4. 04Pills, hairlines and one sharp tile: one clear window on what the surface covers.

The living world

From above, never at eye level. Water draws the line, the green is deep and cool, and the living world, blurred, becomes the ground.

The wounded world

Sharp and real: documentary, never staged, and a place rather than a person. Seen through a tile or the glass, rarely full bleed. Every image credited, every place named.

Type on image

Big, plain type near the edge, shapes cut into the photo, one white frame line, pills floating on the land. The mining report is the point: a gold miner’s green cover, exactly the comfort MRD names.

Illustration

Printed, single-colour marks, made by hand or from real data: rings for time, grain for the cut, codes for nature sold, contours for the land.
