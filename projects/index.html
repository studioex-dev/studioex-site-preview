Participation platformSmallWorld

Our IP · 2026 · Prototype

Small World

A participation platform. One person joins, invites the next, and everyone can see what grew.

Our role
A Studio EX product, designed and built in-house. It grew out of The Six Degrees.
In numbers
A leaf per person, a branch per invitation. Places with fewer than 50 people show as fewer than 50.
Where it stands
Clickable prototype, a 95-feature set and brand guidelines 3.0.

The Cost of War

A live data artwork: what the world spends on violence, set against what the same money could build instead.

Our role
A data artwork by Blueprint, produced by Studio EX. First edition.
In numbers
$691,590 every second: the cost of violence, from the IEP Global Peace Index 2026. $2.89 trillion in military spending in 2025 (SIPRI 2026).
Where it stands
Live and counting: studioex-creative.github.io/cost-of-war.

Mutually Reassured Destruction

A participatory climate artwork that grows with every person who joins. The first Small World campaign.

Our role
Produced by Studio EX, on Small World, our participation platform.
In numbers
Every mark adds to one artwork. One ring for every [1,000] marks (proposed). 100% of public donations go straight to the charities.
Where it stands
Phase 1: building the demo. The record, an interactive data piece, is online now.

Our IP · London · 2027, date to confirm

A Problem Shared

A problem shared is a problem halved. Eight runners, one marathon, 80 kg shared until everyone carries 10 kg, for mental health.

Our role
An artwork by Blueprint, produced by Studio EX: the run, a series of short films and an installation.
In numbers
8 runners. 42.2 km. 80 kg, halved at 10, 16 and 24 km down to 10 kg each.
Where it stands
Concept and partnership deck done. Proposed for World Mental Health Day, 10 October 2027; date to confirm.

Olha Pra Mim

Look at me: 4,000+ looks recorded since 2014, from São Paulo to the Xingu. Everyone deserves to be seen.
