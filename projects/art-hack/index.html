Hackathons for creatives.

Art Hack is a one-day creative sprint where artists form interdisciplinary teams and make new work from scratch around a shared theme.

The art world deserves hackathons.

How it works

One theme. One day. No excuses.

  1. 01

    Form a team

    Artists from different disciplines form small creative teams around the theme.

  2. 02

    Make from scratch

    Every work is made on the day, from nothing.

  3. 03

    Show the work

    Teams share the piece and the process, then hear what people think.
