Our story

So far

From one studio in London to rooms in Chennai.

  1. 2023

    Studio EX opens

    Founded in London by Gregory Taylor. Backed by friends and family.

  2. Jul 2024

    The Six Degrees

    Prototype 01 of a live artwork that maps how people are connected.

  3. Nov 2024

    Olha Pra Mim

    With São Paulo photographer Thiago Santos: every look deserves to be seen.

  4. Mar 2025

    The Six Degrees

    Prototype 02 of the artwork.

  5. Apr 2025

    Creative Research Group

    Three nights of art, music and fashion at The Loop, Hackney Wick.

  6. Dec 2025

    FOMO 001

    Our first open-call show: 44 artists, Hackney.

  7. Mar 2026

    Innovate X

    Greg speaks at the Science Museum. Catalyst, a network for creatives, is built in two days.

  8. Apr 2026

    LXW26

    London Experience Week. Telar’s first room, then called Orbit: 339 people, 949 connections.

  9. May 2026

    FOMO 002

    Day & Night: 60 artists, Marble Arch.

  10. Jul 2026

    The Cost of War

    Live online: military spending set against the UN goals.

  11. Aug 2026

    India

    EEMAGINE in Chennai, Telar’s first show abroad: 5,200+ connections in 3 days.

  12. Sep 2026

    Telar

    Our networking work becomes one product, Telar, now live at telar.uk.

  13. Now

    Three tracks

    Studio EX becomes a creative technology studio: for hire, our IP, touring events.

Next: Parlour, a new night, plans to open at FlipDog in December 2026. Art Hack’s next London sprint is planned for January 2027, and Assembly for a tour of London, Manchester and Leeds in 2027.
