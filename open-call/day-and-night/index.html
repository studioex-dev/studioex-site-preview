Key dates

Six dates, all in 2026. The show opened with Show & Tell on 29 May and the opening night on 30 May. Submissions stayed open until 12:00 on 19 May.

The brief

Exploring human connection, relationships, interaction and social constructs, under the theme Day & Night.

Interpret the theme freely, however it fits your practice or past work. Every medium is welcome: fine art, sculpture, installation, performance, sound, digital, graffiti.

The show ran in the build-up to The Six Degrees, Studio EX and Blueprint’s interactive artwork mapping human connection.
